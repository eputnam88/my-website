about

Hi! I recently graduated from UC Berkeley with a degree in microbial biology. I write about science, politics, and anything else true in whatever format fits best. My work has explored fentanyl and the DEA, rocketry, controversial medical research, campaign financing, and music.

In my free time I play violin for Chalk Talk (we’re going on tour!), read, and write everything down.
