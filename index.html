about

Hi! I recently graduated from UC Berkeley with a degree in microbial biology. I write about science, music, politics, and anything else true in whatever format fits best. My work has explored fentanyl and the DEA, ICE protests, rocketry, controversial medical research, campaign financing, and music. I also led a team producing live sessions and interviews with Bay Area bands.

I’ve guest lectured at Freie Universität Berlin and presented my research at UC Berkeley.

In my free time I play violin for Chalk Talk (we’re going on tour!), read, and write everything down.
